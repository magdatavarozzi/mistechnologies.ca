Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information collected

The contact form collects a name, an email address, the service of interest and a message. No account is created and no payment information is collected.

Purpose

This information is used only to reply to the message and to follow up on the request. It is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Service providers

Form messages are delivered by Formspree, a form service based in the United States, so this information may be stored outside Québec. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the visitor’s IP address to deliver them.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and follow up, then deleted.

Cookies

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. The language choice is saved only in the visitor’s own browser.

Access, correction and deletion

Anyone may ask to see, correct or delete their personal information by writing to the address below.

Person responsible

Requests about personal information are handled by the person responsible for the protection of personal information at MISTechnologies.
